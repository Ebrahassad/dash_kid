## Everything is now wired end-to-end

- Object pooling (`ObjectPool`) is actually used by `ObstacleEngine` and
  `ItemEngine` — instances are recycled, not reallocated every spawn.
- All 5 `ObstacleMotion` behaviors are implemented and actually assigned to
  at least one obstacle type (static, movingLaneChange, movingTowardPlayer,
  crossLane, sideAppear).
- Achievements (`first_run`, `cans_100/500/1000`, `perfect_level`,
  `no_hit`, `speed_runner`, `world_complete`, `all_worlds_complete`) are
  evaluated automatically in `GameProgressManager.recordLevelResult`.
- `CollisionEngine.checkCheckpointReached` is used by `LevelEngine`
  instead of duplicating the comparison inline.
- `android/` platform folder is present and wired to the app (package
  `com.pepsirunner.game`, launcher icons at all densities). See
  `START_HERE.md` for the one required first-run step.
